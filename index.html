Frequently Asked Questions About Fitosauna Steam Saunas

Is it possible to view the Fitosauna products?

Yes, our saunas are stored in our warehouse in Riga. You can schedule a viewing by contacting us in advance.

How do I pay for a purchase?

We accept bank transfers, credit cards, and cash payments. Detailed payment options are provided at checkout or upon request.

How to install, maintain, and disassemble saunas?

Installation is straightforward — the sauna comes fully assembled and ready to use. Simply place it in your chosen location and plug it in. Maintenance is minimal: wipe down after each session and clean periodically. When moving, the sauna can be disassembled into panels for easy transport. To learn more, watch our video guides.

Will it be hot enough in the sauna?

Absolutely. With 99% humidity and moist steam, the sauna feels intensely warm even at moderate temperatures. Most users find 45°C very comfortable, and the heat is distributed evenly for a deeply relaxing experience.

Can the sauna be placed outdoors?

Yes, Fitosauna can be used outdoors in covered or sheltered areas. However, we recommend protecting it from direct rain and extreme cold to prolong its lifespan.

Can the sauna be relocated?

Yes. The sauna is designed to be portable. It can be disassembled into panels and reassembled at a new location with minimal effort.

Will the steam not damage the room’s ceilings or walls?

No. The steam remains inside the enclosed cabin and does not escape into the room. The head-out design ensures that moisture is contained within the sauna body.

Does the sauna need to be connected to the water supply or drainage?

No, the Fitosauna does not require any plumbing connections. It operates entirely with its built-in steam generator. All you need is a standard electrical outlet.

What if my doorways are narrow?

The sauna is designed with a modular panel system, which means it can be disassembled into compact sections. Each panel fits through standard doorways, even narrow ones.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery within Latvia takes 3–5 business days. International shipping times vary by destination. Contact us for a precise estimate based on your location.
